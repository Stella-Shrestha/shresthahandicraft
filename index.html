Crafted with Heritage, Made to Last

What makes our sacred sculptures different is the craftsmanship, patience and Nepalese heritage behind every piece we create.

Authentic Nepalese Craftsmanship

Each sculpture is created in Nepal using traditional metalworking techniques shaped by generations of artistic knowledge and heritage.

Handcrafted, Never Mass Produced

Every statue passes through skilled artisan hands. From wax modeling to finishing, no piece is simply taken from a production line.

Meticulous Detail & Proportion

Facial expressions, hand gestures, ornaments and traditional proportions are carefully refined to preserve the character of each deity.

Premium Metal & Finishes

We work with carefully selected bronze, brass, copper and refined finishes to create sculptures with depth, character and lasting beauty.

Custom Commissions

From intimate altar pieces to large sacred sculptures, we can create custom dimensions, finishes and specifications for individual commissions.

Worldwide Delivery

Your sculpture is carefully inspected, protected and prepared for secure international delivery from our workshop to your collection or sacred space.
