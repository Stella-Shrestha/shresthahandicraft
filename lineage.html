40+ Year Newar Heritage

The Lineage of Tamrakar Master Sculptors

Patan (Lalitpur), the ancient City of Fine Arts, has preserved the lost-wax casting philosophy (*Cire Perdue*) since the Licchavi and Malla dynasties.

Founded by the Tamrakar clan over four decades ago, Shrestha Handicraft stands as one of the last authentic sacred metallurgy ateliers maintaining uninterrupted adherence to Sanskrit iconometric treatises.

"Without correct measurement, the deity cannot abide in the image. Iconometry is the breath of the bronze."
— Samarangana Sutradhara, Sloka 108

Unlike modern commercial mass-casting, every statue commissioned in our Patan foundry begins with hand-carved beeswax blended with sal tree resin. Once melted away in the crucible furnace at 1,080°C, the void receives the molten Ashtadhatu alloy — creating a singular, unrepeatable masterpiece.

Atelier Verification Standards

  • 01
    Alloy Spectrometry Assay: Certified breakdown of gold, silver, copper, and trace sacred elements.
  • 02
    Angula Ratio Certification: High-precision laser measurement verifying symmetry tolerances under 0.2mm.
  • 03
    Consecration Chamber: Statues prepared with hollow core chambers for sacred mantra scroll insertion by Buddhist monks.
