01 - OUR STORY

A Craft Passed Down.

In the historic artisan quarters of Patan, metalwork is more than a profession. It is a tradition shaped by generations of knowledge, patience and devotion.

Shrestha Handicraft was established with a simple purpose: to preserve this living tradition while creating sculptures that can be appreciated by collectors, devotees and art lovers around the world.

Each piece begins as an idea and gradually takes form through modelling, mould preparation, metal casting, chasing, polishing and finishing. The process is deliberately detailed because craftsmanship cannot be rushed.

"A meaningful sculpture is not simply made. It is patiently shaped through knowledge, proportion and hand."
